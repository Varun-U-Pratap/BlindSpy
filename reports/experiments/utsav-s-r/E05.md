### E05 — Random Forest, 300 trees (2026-10-28, utsav-s-r)

Hypothesis: the E02 decision tree is unpruned (depth 16-35), so it fits noise in its training
rows. A Random Forest averages 300 such trees, each grown on a bootstrap sample with a random
subset of features at every split, which lowers that variance. It should clearly beat the
single tree, and since the current best (signed-log KNN, E04) is only 0.006-0.038 above the
tree, the forest should also pass it in at least half of the tasks.

Threshold (set before running, never moved afterwards), validation macro F1 at 15 s; reference
values from `reports/baseline_tables.md` (E02) and `reports/phase3_tables.md` (current best
after E04):
- (a) the forest beats the E02 tree (A1 0.8901, A2-VPN 0.8247, A2-NonVPN 0.8294, B 0.7585) by
  at least 0.03 in at least 3 of the 4 tasks;
- (b) the forest beats the current best, E04-knn_log_standard (A1 0.9048, A2-VPN 0.8544,
  A2-NonVPN 0.8670, B 0.7644), in at least 2 of the 4 tasks (any margin).
Each part gets its own verdict.

Winner rule (decided 28 Oct, written here before running): a model whose 15 s validation macro
F1 beats that task's current best by at least 0.03 is a winner and is rerun at 30, 60 and
120 s with its 15 s settings. Why 0.03: it is above the largest Phase 2 CV standard deviation
(0.025), so a smaller gain could be noise from the split; a fixed number set before running
keeps the choice honest and repeatable. The current best is the highest 15 s validation macro F1
whatever the margin; the 0.03 only decides the reruns.

Change vs the current best (one change: the model family): model `rf` =
`RandomForestClassifier(n_estimators=300, random_state=42, n_jobs=1)`; every other setting is
scikit-learn's default (Gini, no depth limit, `max_features="sqrt"`, bootstrap, no class
weights). No scaling (trees don't need it) and no log. One thread per model, the 5 CV folds in
parallel (logged as `model_n_jobs` and `cv_n_jobs`). Configs:
`configs/experiments/E05_rf_<task>_15s.yaml` (`scripts/make_configs.py`). Protocol as before:
5-fold stratified CV on train, fit on train, score on validation; test set untouched. Models
saved with joblib compression level 3. Data version v1.

New model type: the first run's logged fields are checked by query (plan: pilot check for every
new model type), including the forest's complexity (number of trees, mean and max depth, nodes).

Storage (plan): after the runs, mlruns/ is measured per model type with compression on, and the
project total is projected again.

Result: 4 runs at 15 s from dev commit 817757f and 3 winner reruns (B at 30, 60, 120 s) from
dev commit 016ccd3 (all FINISHED, committed and pushed, data v1). Numbers below come from
`reports/phase3_tables.md` (generated by `scripts/results_tables.py --phase 3` from
`reports/history.csv`).

New-model-type check (by query, first run E05-rf-A1-15s): every field of the tracking table is
logged, plus the forest's complexity (n_trees 300, mean / max depth, total and mean nodes),
impurity feature importance, `model_n_jobs` 1 and `cv_n_jobs` -1. Nothing missing.

Validation macro F1 at 15 s:

| Task | E02 tree | current best before (E04 signed-log KNN) | E05 Random Forest | gain over best | winner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 0.8901 | 0.9048 | 0.9203 | +0.0156 | no |
| A2-VPN | 0.8247 | 0.8544 | 0.8679 | +0.0135 | no |

| B | 0.7585 | 0.7644 | 0.8147 | +0.0503 | yes |

- The forest is the new current best in all 4 tasks. Gains over the single E02 tree: +0.0302
  (A1), +0.0432 (A2-VPN), +0.0647 (A2-NonVPN), +0.0561 (B).
- Only B clears the 0.03 winner margin. Winner reruns for B (same settings): 0.8114 at 30 s,
  0.7771 at 60 s, 0.7835 at 120 s, against the best other run at each timeout (E02 tree 0.7476,
  0.7058, 0.7158): +0.064, +0.071, +0.068. The gain holds at every timeout and is largest
  where the tree was weakest. B still scores lower at 60 s and 120 s than at 15 s and 30 s, as
  every model did in Phase 2; with the class-mix differences (E00 point 10) and the different
  60 s VOIP/FT flows (E00 point 15) this is not read as a timeout effect.
- CV agrees with validation: |CV - validation| <= 0.027 in all 7 runs, CV std 0.0049-0.0136.
- Worst class by F1: VPN-CHAT in A2-VPN (0.71), STREAMING in A2-NonVPN (0.81), VPN-MAIL in B 15 s
  (0.62). In B at 120 s STREAMING drops to 0.49. Best: VOIP / VPN-VOIP (0.93-1.00).
- Complexity: 300 trees of mean depth 20.3-26.5 (deepest tree 34), 249,282 nodes (A2-NonVPN 15 s) to
  1,023,108 nodes (B 15 s).
- Cost: training 2.0-6.1 s on one thread (5-fold CV 3.2-7.1 s with the folds in parallel),
  prediction 21.9-32.7 ms per 1,000 flows (2.0 to 3.3 times the signed-log KNN of E04 on the same task). Model 28.7-171.9
  MiB uncompressed, 5.3-22.4 MiB as saved with compression level 3 (4.8 to 7.7 times smaller).
  Memory: tracemalloc sees only 1.4-2.5 MiB, but the child-process RSS of a fit is 33.1-187.9
  MiB. The trees live in memory that tracemalloc doesn't track, which is why the setup commit
  added the RSS measure.

Storage (plan: measure with compression on, per model type; table "Storage of the Phase 3
runs"): the 7 forest runs take 102.4 MiB in all, at most 22.6 MiB per run (model file 22.4 MiB,
B 15 s). The signed-log KNN runs take at most 1.6 MiB each. Whole store after these runs:
artifacts 390.4 MiB, mlflow.db 11.2 MiB. Projection: the 260 ensemble runs estimated in E03 at
about 11 MiB each (the mean compressed forest file over the four 15 s tasks: 9.4, 8.4, 5.3 and
22.4 MiB) come to about 2.9 GiB. That is far below the plan's 20 GB warning line, so nothing
needs to be raised with the user. E06 and E07 re-measure with boosting and tuned sizes.

Verdict: (a) supported (forest >= tree + 0.03 in 4 of 4 tasks; smallest gain 0.0302 on A1).
(b) supported (forest above the E04 signed-log KNN in 4 of 4 tasks).

Decision: keep the forest as the current best in all four tasks; E06 (boosting) is compared
against it. E07 tunes the best 2 of Random Forest / XGBoost / LightGBM per task (after E06) plus
the signed-log KNN. Next: E06.
