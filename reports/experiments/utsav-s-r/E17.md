### E17 — Equal tuning budget for every method type (2026-10-28, utsav-s-r)

Why this experiment exists (decided 28 Oct after E10, user OK; plan item "Decided 28 Oct, after
E10"): Phase 4 scores a comparison set, the best validation variant of each of the 7 method types
per task. Up to now only some types were tuned: E07 searched XGBoost and KNN in all tasks,
LightGBM in A2-VPN, A2-NonVPN and B, and Random Forest in A1. The decision tree, logistic
regression and SVM ran with defaults only. A comparison of tuned against untuned models would
favour the tuned types for the effort they got, not for what they can do (the weakness we cite
for Isnan et al.). E17 gives every remaining type the same search E07 used, so each type enters
the comparison with the same budget.

Hypothesis: tuning helps every type, most of all logistic regression and SVM. Their E03 defaults
scored only 0.30-0.60 macro F1 because the raw timing features span six or more orders of
magnitude (E04 showed that a signed log fixes this for KNN). Even tuned, they should stay below
the gradient-boosted models, so the overall best per task does not change.

Threshold (set before running, never moved afterwards), validation macro F1 at 15 s; reference
values from `reports/phase3_tables.md` and `reports/baseline_tables.md`:
- (a) in at least 12 of the 16 searches the tuned model scores above its untuned version (E02
  tree, E03 logistic regression, E03 SVM, E05 Random Forest, E06 LightGBM);
- (b) after E17, the best run per task (highest 15 s validation macro F1 of all selection
  experiments) is still an XGBoost or LightGBM model in at least 3 of the 4 tasks;
- (c) the searches choose the signed log for logistic regression and SVM in at least 6 of the 8
  searches.
Each part gets its own verdict.

Winner rule (decided 28 Oct, written here before running): a model whose 15 s validation macro
F1 beats that task's current best by at least 0.03 is a winner and is rerun at 30, 60 and
120 s with its 15 s settings (nothing is re-tuned per timeout: that would be a different, 4
times costlier experiment). Why 0.03: it is above the largest Phase 2 CV standard deviation
(0.025), so a smaller gain could be noise from the split; a fixed number set before running
keeps the choice honest and repeatable. The current best is the highest 15 s validation macro
F1 whatever the margin; the 0.03 only decides the reruns. (The final choice per task no longer
uses the 0.03: it is the highest validation macro F1, decided 28 Oct after E10.)

Change vs the untuned runs (one change: the hyperparameters, chosen by search), 16 searches:
- decision tree, logistic regression, SVM-RBF: all 4 tasks;
- Random Forest: A2-VPN, A2-NonVPN, B (A1 was searched in E07);
- LightGBM: A1 (the other tasks were searched in E07).
Each search as E07: Optuna `TPESampler(seed=42)`, 50 trials run one after another, each trial
scored by the mean macro F1 of 5-fold stratified CV on the training rows (folds in parallel, each
model on one thread). Validation and test rows are never seen by the search. The best trial's
settings are retrained on all training rows and scored on validation like any other run and
saved; trials keep settings and scores only (nested runs tagged `trial=True`, protocol
`optuna_trial`, kept out of the result tables). The retrained run's CV mean must equal the best
trial's exactly.

Search ranges (user OK 28 Oct; written into `scripts/make_configs.py` and every
`configs/experiments/E17_<model>_<task>_15s.yaml` before running; Random Forest and LightGBM
use exactly the E07 ranges, so their searches match E07's):
- Decision tree: criterion gini or entropy; max_depth 3-40 or no limit; min_samples_leaf 1-50;
  ccp_alpha (cost-complexity pruning, the nearest scikit-learn has to C4.5 pruning)
  1e-6-1e-2 (log scale). A monotone transform such as the signed log cannot change a tree's
  splits, so it is not searched.
- Logistic regression: signed log before StandardScaler yes or no; C 1e-3-1e3 (log scale);
  max_iter fixed at 1000 as in E03; L2 penalty, lbfgs solver (scikit-learn defaults).
- SVM-RBF: signed log before StandardScaler yes or no; C 1e-2-1e3 (log scale); gamma
  1e-4-1e1 (log scale); cache_size fixed at 1000 MB (speed only, results unchanged).
- Random Forest (as E07): n_estimators 100-1000; max_depth 5-40 or no limit;
  min_samples_leaf 1-10; max_features sqrt, log2 or a fraction 0.3-1.0.
- LightGBM (as E07): n_estimators 100-1000; learning_rate 0.01-0.3 (log scale); num_leaves
  15-255; subsample 0.5-1.0 with subsample_freq fixed at 1; colsample_bytree 0.5-1.0;
  min_child_samples 1-50.
The signed log is offered to logistic regression and SVM as a choice inside the search (not as
a separate experiment like E04) because both, like KNN, depend on distances or linear scales
that the raw feature ranges distort; the search decides per task. Balancing (E08) is not part of
E17: it stays only on the models E08 ran on, and the comparison table shows each model's
balancing.

Cost check before running (throwaway fits, nothing logged): one SVM fit on the 12,651 B
training rows takes 2.2-3.9 s at the edges of the range (C 1000 with gamma 10 or 1e-4, C 0.01
with gamma 1e-4, signed log with C 1000), so no search is expected to stall.

Protocol as E07: data v1, 15 s, test set untouched. New registry option: `signed_log` for
logistic regression and SVM (`src/blindspy/models.py`); without it the pipelines are exactly the
E03 ones.

Result: 16 searches from dev commit 7aa4658 (hypothesis and configs committed 22 s before the
first run): 16 retrained runs and 800 trial runs, all FINISHED, committed and pushed, data v1;
49.1 minutes of search in all. Numbers below come from `reports/phase3_tables.md` (tables "E17
searches", "E17 at 15 s against the best run before it", "E17 run details", "Current best at 15
s after E17"), generated by `scripts/results_tables.py --phase 3` from `reports/history.csv`.
For all 16 searches the retrained run's CV macro F1 equals the best trial's exactly.

- SVM gains most: +0.32 to +0.53. Every SVM and logistic-regression search chose the signed log
  (8 of 8), with large C (5.6-879) and gamma 0.18-2.77. Tuned SVM is now close to the boosters:
  A1 0.9259 (best 0.9308), A2-NonVPN 0.9058 (best 0.9075), A2-VPN 0.8707 (0.8830), B 0.8242
  (0.8428). Its E03 score (0.30-0.60) came from the raw feature scales, not from the method.
- Logistic regression gains +0.07 to +0.35 but stays far below every other type (0.59-0.80): a
  linear boundary does not fit these data, even after the signed log.
- Decision tree: the searches settle on (almost) the E02 tree, an unpruned entropy tree (largest
  ccp_alpha chosen 0.00066). Change +0.0000 to +0.0044; in A2-NonVPN and B the tuned tree scores
  exactly the same as E02 (equal to all digits). More pruning did not help on these data.
- Random Forest (A2-VPN, A2-NonVPN, B): +0.0025, -0.0111 and +0.0020. In A2-NonVPN the tuned forest
  (132 trees) has a higher CV score (0.9063 vs 0.9048) but a lower validation score: the best of
  50 CV estimates is optimistic, as seen in E07.
- LightGBM A1: +0.0127, to 0.9308, now the best A1 model (above E06 XGBoost 0.9271 by +0.0037,
  no winner).
- No winner (largest gain over the best before E17: +0.0037), so nothing is rerun at 30, 60 or
  120 s. Current best at 15 s after E17: A1 E17 LightGBM 0.9308, A2-VPN E08 LightGBM + class
  weights 0.8830, A2-NonVPN E08 LightGBM + oversampling 0.9075, B E07 XGBoost 0.8428.
- Convergence: logistic regression keeps E03's max_iter = 1000 (fixed before running). In the
  search, lbfgs stopped at that limit in some trials (216 ConvergenceWarnings in the batch log).
  Refitting the 4 chosen settings: A1, A2-VPN and B converge (260, 800 and 716 iterations);
  A2-NonVPN (C = 985) stops at 1,000, so its score may be slightly below what more iterations
  would give. All 4 chosen SVMs converge. Not changed after the fact; noted as a limitation.
- Cost (table "E17 run details"): tuned SVM predicts slowly (47.6-302.8 ms per 1,000 flows; 1,061-
  5,711 support vectors) but is small (0.17-1.31 MiB as saved); tuned LightGBM A1 7.1 MiB and
  191 ms per 1,000 flows; tuned Random Forest B 340.4 MiB uncompressed / 45.8 MiB as saved
  (708 trees, 2.03 million nodes), the largest model file so far.

Storage: the 16 retrained E17 runs take 79.4 MiB (forest 68.0, LightGBM 7.2, SVM 2.9, tree 0.7,
logistic regression 0.5); trials save no model. Whole store now: artifacts 664.1 MiB, mlflow.db
26.1 MiB (1,756 runs). Far below the plan's 20 GB line. Flag for Phase 4: the E17 Random Forest
for B (45.8 MiB as saved) is the best B forest, so it is the forest in the B comparison set; it
is under GitHub's 50 MB warning size, but close.

Verdict: (a) supported (tuned above untuned in 13 of 16 searches; 12 needed; not above: Random
Forest A2-NonVPN -0.0111, tree A2-NonVPN and B 0.0000). (b) supported (the best run per task is
XGBoost or LightGBM in 4 of 4 tasks). (c) supported (signed log chosen in 8 of 8 logistic-
regression and SVM searches).

Decision: every method type now has the same 50-trial budget per task, so the comparison set can
be built fairly. The final choice (highest validation macro F1, decided 28 Oct after E10) and the
28-model comparison set are written to configs/final/ next.
