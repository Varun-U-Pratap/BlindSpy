### E06 — Gradient boosting: XGBoost and LightGBM with default settings (2026-10-28, utsav-s-r)

Hypothesis: gradient-boosted trees are usually the strongest model family on tables of
numbers. Each tree corrects the errors of the trees before it, which should help the classes
the forest still mixes up (VPN-CHAT, STREAMING, the mail classes). With default settings they
should at least match the 300-tree Random Forest (E05), and XGBoost and LightGBM, which use
similar algorithms, should score close to each other.

Threshold (set before running, never moved afterwards), validation macro F1 at 15 s, against
the current best after E05, the Random Forest (A1 0.9203, A2-VPN 0.8679, A2-NonVPN 0.8941,
B 0.8147; `reports/phase3_tables.md`):
- (a) the better of XGBoost and LightGBM beats the forest in at least 2 of the 4 tasks (any
  margin);
- (b) XGBoost and LightGBM are within 0.02 of each other in at least 3 of the 4 tasks.
Each part gets its own verdict.

Winner rule (decided 28 Oct, written here before running): a model whose 15 s validation macro
F1 beats that task's current best by at least 0.03 is a winner and is rerun at 30, 60 and
120 s with its 15 s settings. Why 0.03: it is above the largest Phase 2 CV standard deviation
(0.025), so a smaller gain could be noise from the split; a fixed number set before running
keeps the choice honest and repeatable. The current best is the highest 15 s validation macro F1
whatever the margin; the 0.03 only decides the reruns.

What E07 takes from this: per task, the best 2 of Random Forest (E05), XGBoost and LightGBM by
15 s validation macro F1 are tuned with Optuna (decided 28 Oct), plus the signed-log KNN.

Change vs the current best (one change: the model family): model `xgb` = `XGBLabelled`
(`src/blindspy/models.py`), XGBoost's `XGBClassifier` with every setting at its default
(100 rounds, learning rate 0.3, max depth 6, histogram trees) except `n_jobs=1` and
`random_state=42`; the wrapper only encodes class names to 0..k-1 for XGBoost and back. Model
`lgbm` = `LGBMClassifier` with defaults (100 rounds, learning rate 0.1, 31 leaves) except
`n_jobs=1`, `random_state=42` and `verbose=-1` (silences its log, no effect on the model). No
scaling and no log (trees). One thread per model, the 5 CV folds in parallel. Configs:
`configs/experiments/E06_{xgb,lgbm}_<task>_15s.yaml` (`scripts/make_configs.py`). Protocol as
before; test set untouched. Models saved with compression level 3. Data version v1.

New model types: the first run of each is checked by query (all tracking-table fields, plus
number of trees, mean and max depth and nodes read from the booster's own tree dump). The
memory measure that matters here is the child-process RSS: XGBoost and LightGBM allocate in C++,
which tracemalloc does not see.

Result: 8 runs at 15 s from dev commit 4308ecb (all FINISHED, committed and pushed, data v1).
Numbers below come from `reports/phase3_tables.md` (generated by
`scripts/results_tables.py --phase 3` from `reports/history.csv`).

New-model-type check (by query, first runs E06-xgb-A1-15s and E06-lgbm-A1-15s, commit 0658b61):
every field of the tracking table was logged, with tree counts, depths and nodes. One gap: the
`estimator` param is scikit-learn's repr, which is cut at 700 characters; for XGBoost it shows
every default as None and the cut hid `random_state=42`. Fixed (commit b38cd83, merged as
4308ecb): a new param `estimator_params` holds every explicit setting as JSON (XGBoost:
n_jobs 1, random_state 42, objective), and XGBoost's resolved settings (defaults filled in, e.g.
max_depth 6, eta 0.3) are saved as `model/xgboost_config.json`. All 8 runs were then made from
4308ecb; the rerun of E06-xgb-A1-15s gave the same validation macro F1 (0.92707) as the first.

Validation macro F1 at 15 s:

| Task | current best before (E05 forest) | E06 XGBoost | E06 LightGBM | gain of the better one | winner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 0.9203 | 0.9271 | 0.9181 | +0.0067 | no |
| A2-VPN | 0.8679 | 0.8758 | 0.8694 | +0.0079 | no |
| A2-NonVPN | 0.8941 | 0.9015 | 0.8983 | +0.0075 | no |
| B | 0.8147 | 0.8303 | 0.8201 | +0.0156 | no |

- XGBoost with default settings is the new current best in all 4 tasks. No model clears the
  0.03 winner margin, so there are no reruns at other timeouts.
- XGBoost beats LightGBM in all 4 tasks, by 0.0032-0.0102. LightGBM is below the forest on A1
  (-0.0022) and above it elsewhere.
- CV agrees with validation (|CV - validation| <= 0.024; CV std 0.0025-0.0117). On A2-NonVPN
  both boosters score about 0.02 higher in CV than on validation, as the forest did (+0.011).
- Worst class by F1: VPN-CHAT in A2-VPN (0.72 both), STREAMING in A2-NonVPN (XGBoost 0.78,
  LightGBM 0.82), VPN-MAIL in B (XGBoost 0.66, LightGBM 0.62). Best: VOIP / VPN-VOIP (0.99-1.00).
- Complexity: 100 boosting rounds, one tree per class per round in multi-class tasks (100 trees
  for A1, 700 for A2, 1,400 for B). XGBoost trees have depth 6 (its default), 7,500-66,808 nodes
  in all; LightGBM grows 31-leaf trees leaf-wise, mean depth 11.3-13.4 (max 30), 6,100-85,400
  nodes.
- Cost: training 0.19-2.3 s (a tenth to a third of the forest's time), prediction 1.1-11.8 ms
  per 1,000 flows for XGBoost and 5.1-98.3 ms for LightGBM. Models are 0.32-4.60 MiB
  uncompressed and 0.12-2.01 MiB as saved, against 5-22 MiB for the forest. Memory: tracemalloc
  0.4-13.9 MiB, child-process RSS 7.4-41.7 MiB.

Verdict: (a) supported (the better booster beats the forest in 4 of 4 tasks; gains 0.0067,
0.0079, 0.0075, 0.0156). (b) supported (XGBoost and LightGBM within 0.02 in 4 of 4 tasks).

Decision: XGBoost (defaults) is the current best in all four tasks. E07 tunes, per task, the best
2 of Random Forest / XGBoost / LightGBM by 15 s validation macro F1 (table "E07 scope"): A1
XGBoost and Random Forest; A2-VPN, A2-NonVPN and B XGBoost and LightGBM. Plus signed-log KNN
(standard scaling, the better E04 variant in every task). That is 12 searches of 50 trials.
