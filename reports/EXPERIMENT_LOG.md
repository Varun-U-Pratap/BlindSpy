### E00 — Data findings and data v1 (2026-10-27, utsav-s-r)

Hypothesis (from the plan, written before the check): the ARFF `@ATTRIBUTE` names may not match
the data order. In A1 15 s row 1, `total_fiat` = 16 and `total_biat` = 4 in a 9.37 s flow, which
looks wrong; the paper's Table 2 and the authors' 2016 poster list fiat/biat as min, mean, max,
std, with no totals. Candidates: `total_*` are packet counts, `std_*`, or something else.

Threshold: no numeric threshold was written before the check. Decision rule used: a name list
is accepted for a file when every structural identity (min <= mean <= max per group, 0 <= std <=
max - min, gaps x (packets - 1) = duration) holds on 100% of applicable rows, allowing for the
source's rounding. A list is rejected when the identities fail on most rows.

Change: none to models. Built the parser (`src/blindspy/data.py`), the checks
(`src/blindspy/checks.py`), `scripts/prepare_data.py` and `src/blindspy/splits.py`, then data
version v1. Config: `configs/base.yaml` (`cleaning:`). Commit 0a36896. Data version v1.
Every number below is in `data/manifest.json` (keys named in brackets) or
`data/processed/v1/cleaning_log.csv`.

Result:

1. **Two header layouts.** 17 files use header "T" (`total_fiat`, `total_biat`, no std); the
   three 60 s files of A1 and B (incl. B-AllinOne) use header "S" (`std_fiat`, `std_biat`, no
   totals), the paper's list. [`files[].header`]
2. **Column-name check** [`column_name_check`]. Read under the S names, every A1 and B file
   passes fiat/biat min <= mean <= max and 0 <= std <= max - min on 100% of rows. Read under
   the T names, the same files pass min <= mean <= max on 0.00-0.02% and the packet-count
   identity on 0.00-0.05%. A2 files are the reverse: under the T names min <= mean <= max and
   "total/mean + 1, forward + backward = packet count (+-2%)" pass on 100%. Under the S names
   std <= max - min passes on 0%. The plan's packet-count test (mean_fiat x (total_fiat - 1)
   = duration +-10%) passes on 0-2% of rows in every file, so `total_*` are not packet counts.
   Verdict: A1 and B hold the paper's features, and 9 of their 12 headers shift the fiat/biat
   names by one pair. A2 holds `total_fiat`/`total_biat` (sum of the gaps) exactly as named.
3. **Final COLUMN_MAP** (`src/blindspy/data.py`; header name -> real content):
   - A1 and B files with header T: `total_fiat`->`min_fiat`, `total_biat`->`min_biat`,
     `min_fiat`->`max_fiat`, `min_biat`->`max_biat`, `max_fiat`->`mean_fiat`,
     `max_biat`->`mean_biat`, `mean_fiat`->`std_fiat`, `mean_biat`->`std_biat`. The other
     15 names are unchanged.
   - A1 and B files with header S, and A2 files: names unchanged.
   - Checked on the cleaned data: every structural check passes on 100% of rows in all 16 files
     [`tasks.*.consistency_checks`]. Bytes per packet falls in 40-1,500 B for 96.1-99.3% of
     flows; the rest are header-only packets (down to 30 B) and segments merged at capture
     (up to about 7 kB).
4. **Same flows in all families** [`family_match`]. At every timeout, 100% of A1 rows match an
   A2 row on 7 integer columns. A1 labels equal the A2 VPN/Non-VPN labels key by key, and B
   labels equal the A2 labels (0 disagreeing keys), and all 21 shared columns agree on rows
   with a unique key. B and A1 are identical cell for cell, except A1 120 s
   `flowPktsPerSecond` (10,288 cells). There A1 agrees with A2 on 0.03% of paired rows, while
   B 120 s agrees on 100%. So A1 120 s has one corrupt column.
5. **File-family decision** (user OK, 2026-10-27): each task keeps its own files. A1 and B use
   the paper's 23 features; A2 uses its 23 (totals in place of stds). Nothing is rebuilt. A1
   120 s `flowPktsPerSecond` is restored from B 120 s. Reason: these are the files the paper
   used per scenario, so the paper comparison stays matched. A rebuild from A2 would swap 2 of
   the paper's features, and a 25-feature merge is ambiguous where keys repeat. E10 trains its
   7-class step on B rows split by VPN, so both steps share the A1/B layout.
6. **Odd values** (rules in `configs/base.yaml`, counts per file in the cleaning log and
   `tasks.*.odd_values`). No missing values and no infinities in any file. `-1` means "not
   measurable": min = max = -1 with mean = 0 when a direction has fewer than 2 packets
   (forward: 11.2% of A1 15 s flows) or when there is no active/idle period (59.0% of A1 15 s
   flows). Duration 0 marks single-packet flows (rates 0). Negative `min_flowiat` (down to
   -1,247 us) comes from out-of-order capture timestamps. Every mean, std and rate of 1e7 or
   more is stored to 3 significant figures (100% of such values; integer columns are exact).
   Rule (user OK): keep all values as-is, log every case, no flag columns, nothing set to 0.
7. **Duplicates** [`tasks.*.summary`, `*_per_class`]. Exact duplicates within files: 11,209 of

   1,102 rows in 3-77 groups per file. Rule (user OK): drop exact duplicates before splitting,
